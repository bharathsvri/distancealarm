Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 Developer: B47 Tech Application: Distance Alarm (com.b47tech.distancealarm)

Summary: Distance Alarm is built on a 100% offline-first, on-device architecture. We do NOT collect, upload, share, or sell your location coordinates or personal information to any cloud servers or third parties.

1. Introduction

B47 Tech ("we", "us", or "our") develops and operates the Distance Alarm mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, processing, and handling of location and device information when you use our application.

By downloading, installing, or using Distance Alarm, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. 100% On-Device Location Computation

The core functionality of Distance Alarm is providing proximity alerts as you travel toward a designated geographic destination.

  • Local Math Processing: All distance formulas and geofencing calculations between your device's current location and your destination are performed purely in-memory on your physical device.
  • Zero Remote Transmission: Your exact GPS coordinates, heading, altitude, and velocity are never sent across the internet to our servers or any third-party infrastructure.
  • No Movement History Tracking: Distance Alarm does not log, store, or profile your historical routes or travel timeline. Once an alarm session is completed or stopped, live location readings are immediately discarded from memory.

3. Permissions Requested & Their Purpose

Distance Alarm requests only the minimal system permissions required to deliver accurate arrival alerts:

  • Precise Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) & Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required strictly while an alarm tracking session is active to calculate your remaining distance from the destination.
  • Foreground Service with Location Type (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION): Required to keep location tracking operational while your screen is off, while the device is locked, or while you are multitasking in other apps. An ongoing persistent notification is displayed throughout tracking, allowing you to stop tracking at any moment.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13 and above to show ongoing distance readouts in your status bar and display arrival alert banners.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used to trigger tactile wake-up vibration pulses when reaching distance alert milestones.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Momentarily awakens the device screen when reaching the final destination threshold to ensure you do not miss your arrival.

4. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Distance Alarm stores only the configurations you explicitly choose to create:

  • Saved Destinations: Custom destination names (e.g., "Home", "Central Station"), coordinates, and threshold preferences are stored locally in an on-device SQLite database via Android Room.
  • User Preferences: App preferences (theme selection, default alert distance, audio/vibration toggles) are stored locally in Jetpack DataStore.

This data remains strictly on your device and is never synchronized to cloud accounts.

5. Third-Party Services & Map Tiles

Distance Alarm integrates the Google Maps SDK for Android to render interactive destination maps and markers. Map tile requests are handled directly by Google Play Services under Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because your location data is processed ephemerally in RAM, we maintain no data to delete on remote servers. For local device data:

  • You can delete any bookmarked destination anytime within the app's "Saved Destinations" screen.
  • You can erase all local data immediately by clearing the app's cache and storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

7. Children's Privacy

Distance Alarm is designed for general audiences and commuters (ages 13 and above). We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.
